## Notable Observations

1. **v0.2 rejects 44 filled trades** (30% of fills) due to no structural T1 inside the family band. The rejected set had a *slightly positive* v0.1 counterfactual (+0.012R), so v0.2 is discarding marginally profitable v0.1 trades — but the overall v0.2 accepted book is worse (−0.138R vs −0.050R on the v0.1 accepted book).

2. **The v0.2 exit policy underperforms v0.1 on the same fills** (exit-only delta: −0.138R vs −0.096R on 103 paired exits). The family-specific trails (2.0×ATR trend / 2.5×ATR breakout) and full-exit-at-T1 for MR/rotation/unknown are not adding value on this window.

3. **46 of 101 closed v0.2 trades map to `unknown` family** (the three screens above). Their exit policy is the MR/rotation/unknown rule (100% at T1, 10-day stop), which may explain part of the drag.

4. **Data horizon = 11 days past window_end (need 36)** — this is a censored dry run. The earliest full write is **2026-11-24**.

5. **Disclosed deviation:** H-09 uses signal-close ATR stop (`stop = C − 2.0×ATR`); D-71's engine used fill-based ATR. This is recorded in the run's `design.deviations`.
