# 2026-10-28: Pass Bar — Expectancy, Profit Factor, Loss Control (D-75)

**Scope:** documentation only. Closes P0-4 from D-74. No code, schema or trading
decision changed.

## What was decided

The legacy `VISION.md` pass bar ("hit rate > 50% AND average R > 0.5") was
replaced with:

- expectancy **> 0 after costs**;
- profit factor **≥ 1.3**;
- average loss **≤ 1.0R**;
- at **30+ closed trades**.

Win rate, average win, payoff ratio, MFE capture and a bootstrap 95% CI are
reported, not gated. A risk circuit breaker was added: **8R** peak-to-trough
drawdown → stop new entries and review; **12R** → kill. The kill rule
(expectancy ≤ 0 at 30+ closed trades) is unchanged.

## Why

The user's design brief is patience, fewer/better setups, small controlled
losses, larger winners, trailing/profit-taking, capital preservation. That is a
trend/continuation shape, not a high-win-rate shape. A 40%-WR system with +2R
winners has +0.20R expectancy and PF 1.33, but would fail WR > 50%. A 60%-WR
system with +0.8R winners has +0.08R expectancy and PF 1.20 — it would pass the
old bar and fail the new one. Profit factor encodes the user's actual
requirement: at 40% WR it demands ~2:1 winners; at 55% it demands ~1.06:1.

## Governance

The legacy bar was flagged in the scorecard and pre-registration as **not
applied**; no verdict was issued under it. The new bar is activated before the
30-trade verdict, is not retroactive, and is stricter on loss control and effect
size. The pre-registration amendment log records it as a pre-verdict definition
of a flagged, never-applied bar. No sample reset.
